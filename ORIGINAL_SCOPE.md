# Original scope — «Charity App – Easy Technology» (proposal dated 2026-03-28)

Source: `ORIGINAL_PROPOSAL.pdf` (13 pages, Arabic). Contract timeline: 6 weeks.
Rule (owner, 2026-10-17): anything NOT in this scope is put aside and marked **➕ Addition**; we come back to it later.

## In scope (section numbers of the proposal)
- §3–6 Audiences & accounts: donor, beneficiary, volunteer, staff/organization, **visitor with limited permissions**, marriage-service account. (No separate «general user».)
- §5 Home page / public browsing (logo, sign-in/up, current activities, about/contact/language, stats banner, status indicators, quick sections, media content, motivational elements).
- §7 Beneficiary registration + eligibility review (form, documents, review/approve/reject/edit, duplicate prevention, ID-code instead of name).
- §8 Privacy & dignity (codes not names, hidden sensitive data, safe mediation between donor and case).
- §9 Donations: once/recurring; general or targeted (campaign / project / category); cash; **electronic via the approved payment methods**; in-kind; operational support; reference number; status tracking; notifications; donor history.
- §10 Campaigns & humanitarian projects. §11 Sponsorships & recurring donations (reminders, calendar).
- §12 Activities / news / media. §13 Gamification (stars/badges, history, sharing, motivational elements).
- §14 Products market & productive families (display, link to beneficiary, review/approve, orders + status).
- §15 City guide. §16 Marriage service (private accounts, hidden data, contact only through staff, free/paid).
- §17 Volunteers & partners. §18 Profile, settings, support (contact technical/organization support from inside the app; language; notifications; delete/suspend account).
- §19 Admin dashboard (manage beneficiaries, donors, donations, campaigns, market, city guide, marriage, content, complaints, periodic reports).
- §20 Financial transparency. §21 Notifications. §22 Reports & statistics. §23 Deliverables (mobile app, admin web, systems, reports).

## §24 Explicitly OUT of phase 1
Bank transfer · some government integrations · some deferred extension features · full administrative systems beyond the charity/finance operation.
